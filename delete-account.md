# Delete your InnerWeather account

InnerWeather is a mood journal by **Noronaru**. When you open it for the first time, it gets a random, anonymous identifier with no name or email address (see the [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy)). You have a Google account with us only if you signed in with Google in **Settings → Account**.

**One account for InnerWeather, Hydrorep and Dozelight.** Our apps sign in with the same Google account and share one account. Deleting it in any of them deletes it for all of them, including Hydrorep's cloud backup of your training and the nights Dozelight shares.

**Do you have Plus?** Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play.

## Delete it in the app

1. Open InnerWeather and go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.
3. Choose your Google account once more to confirm it's you.

You need an internet connection. Your account and its data are deleted right away.

## Delete it without the app

Email **noronaru@proton.me** from the email address of your Google account, with the subject **Delete my account**. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address. We delete your account and its data within **30 days** and let you know when it's done.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in account: the email address, name, profile picture link and account identifier Google sign-in gave us
- Your cloud backup of your journal
- The mood scores you shared with Hydrorep and Dozelight
- Everything Hydrorep and Dozelight keep in the same account, such as Hydrorep's cloud backup and the summaries they share
- What our server keeps on your account: the AI usage counts, any reports you sent, and your subscription record at RevenueCat

## What is kept

- **What's on your phone.** Your journal and voice notes stay on your phone. To delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
- **Backup files you exported**, wherever you saved them.
- **Purchases** stay on record with Google Play under its own terms.

We don't keep a copy of deleted data. Google, which hosts our database, may take some time to clear it from its own backup systems.
